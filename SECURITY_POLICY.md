# Financial Security & Payee Protection Protocol

This repository adheres to the **Financial Security, Privacy & Payee Protection Charter**:

1. **No Public PII / VPAs:** Personal UPI IDs, personal phone numbers, and private banking identifiers are never committed to public git history or public docs.
2. **Invoicing via Direct Scope:** Commercial invoices (PayPal, Stripe, Wire/UPI) are provided directly to clients upon written agreement of audit scope.
3. **No Automated Lending / Financial Liability:** Automated agents have zero access to financial or borrowing channels. All outbound transactions and legal agreements require explicit human operator signature and verification.
4. **Fraud & Theft Protection:** VPAs and PayPal addresses are strictly receive-only routing handles. Under banking standards (RBI / PayPal), receiving funds never requires entering a PIN, and loans cannot be taken using a virtual handle alone.
