## Stages

1. **Contacted** — one personalized message sent.
2. **Replied** — the person responded.
3. **Scoped** — repo and scope confirmed in writing.
4. **Quoted** — offer and payment terms sent.
5. **Won** — payment received.
6. **Delivered** — report + walkthrough + re-scan sent.
7. **Lost / No reply** — one follow-up max, then close.

## Rules

- One message per project; one follow-up after 7 days; then stop.
- No mass email, no scraped lists, no issue-comment spam.
- No vulnerability details in the first message.
- Never ask for passwords, 2FA codes, seed phrases, or private keys.
- Record revenue only when it has cleared, not when invoiced.
